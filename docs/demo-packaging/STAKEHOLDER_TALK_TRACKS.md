
Stakeholder Talk Tracks

Status: Talk Track Baseline

CIO / CTO
This platform shows how to move from isolated AI experiments to governed AI operations with visibility into workflows, systems, approvals, and operational readiness.
CISO
This platform makes AI risk review visible by connecting agent workflows, risk tiers, policy gates, approvals, evidence, and enterprise system touchpoints.
Compliance / Audit
This platform creates a structured evidence trail for AI governance decisions, including risk tiering, approval requirements, policy gate outcomes, SOPs, and handoff artifacts.
Platform Engineering
This platform provides an operating model for deploying and supporting AI workflows with runbooks, rollback paths, observability, and cost controls.
Product / Business Leadership
This platform helps teams understand which AI workflows are ready, blocked, approval-bound, or operationally risky before they scale.
