# AI Intake SOP

## Purpose

This SOP defines how Family Dollar business teams submit AI use cases into the AI Governance & Agentic Workflow Platform.

The intake process ensures that business value, system impact, data sensitivity, risk, dependencies, and release expectations are captured before engineering begins.

## Scope

This SOP applies to all AI use cases involving:

- Generative AI
- Agentic workflows
- RAG assistants
- Forecasting models
- Document intelligence
- Customer-facing AI
- Retail-system-integrated AI

## Required Intake Fields

- Business unit
- Requester
- Requested AI use case
- Retail system touched
- Data sensitivity
- Customer data involvement
- Payment data involvement
- Operational impact
- Expected business value
- Model type
- Autonomous action level
- Human approval requirement
- Dependencies
- Target release window

## Intake Procedure

1. Business requester submits the AI request through the intake portal.
2. Platform captures the business requirement and retail system touchpoints.
3. Risk-tiering engine evaluates the request.
4. Governance forum reviews high-risk or restricted requests.
5. Architecture owner confirms integration pattern and dependencies.
6. Approved requests move into prototype planning.
7. Blocked requests remain in governance review until dependencies are resolved.

## Triage Criteria

Requests are prioritized based on:

- Business impact
- Operational criticality
- Risk tier
- Customer impact
- Data sensitivity
- Dependency readiness
- Release-window feasibility
- Supportability

## Governance Principle

AI intake is mandatory before implementation. No AI solution should move to prototype, deployment, or production without intake, risk classification, and architecture review.

## Interview Talk Track

"I would operate intake as the front door for AI delivery. Before a team builds, the platform captures business intent, impacted systems, data sensitivity, customer/payment exposure, autonomy level, dependencies, business value, and release timing. That gives governance and architecture teams the context needed to prioritize safely."
