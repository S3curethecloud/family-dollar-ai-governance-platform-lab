Purpose

Map the actual Command Center sections into executive and operator meaning.

Expected Section Categories

Governance intake
Risk tiering
Agent workflows
Policy gates

Observability and cost controls

Executive Meaning
Executives need to understand governance posture, high-risk AI systems, blocked workflows, approval queues, enterprise exposure, and operational readiness quickly.
Operator Meaning
Operators need traceability from workflow to policy gate, risk tier, API/system contract, approval state, evidence, and runbook.
