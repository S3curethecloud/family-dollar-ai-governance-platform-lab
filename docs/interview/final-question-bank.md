## Strong Answer Formula

Use this structure:

1. State the business goal.
2. Identify the systems involved.
3. Classify the risk.
4. Define the architecture.
5. Define the governance controls.
6. Explain observability.
7. Explain rollback and support.
8. Close with business value.
