# Opening Statement

I built a Family Dollar AI Governance & Agentic Workflow Platform to demonstrate how I would approach this AI Solutions Architect and Governance Lead role.

The platform starts with business intake, maps requests to retail systems, evaluates risk tiers, defines data contracts, runs a governed inventory replenishment agent, enforces policy gates, tracks observability and cost controls, and includes SOPs, runbooks, rollback, support handoff, Docker, CI, and GCP Cloud Run deployment scaffolding.

The main design principle is simple: AI may recommend, summarize, and draft, but it cannot autonomously mutate retail systems, access payment data, bypass human approval, or bypass change control.

This shows how I would build hands-on while also leading governance, architecture, integration, operations, and support transition.
