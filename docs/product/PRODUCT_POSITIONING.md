# SecureTheCloud AI Governance Command Center — Product Positioning

Status: Product Positioning Baseline

## Product Name

```text
SecureTheCloud AI Governance Command Center
Product Category
Enterprise AI governance, risk, control, and evidence platform
Positioning Statement
SecureTheCloud AI Governance Command Center helps enterprises govern AI systems, agents, prompts, model workflows, system integrations, approval gates, risk tiers, evidence trails, and operational handoffs from one command center.
Case Study Source
Family Dollar AI Governance Platform Lab
Product Reuse Decision
Family Dollar remains the implementation case study.
SecureTheCloud AI Governance Command Center becomes the reusable product concept.
Core Platform Promise
Turn AI governance from scattered documents, ad hoc approvals, and unclear ownership into a visible operating model with risk tiers, policy gates, evidence, runbooks, and executive-ready oversight.
Product Pillars
Governance intake
Risk tiering
Policy gates
Agent workflow oversight
Enterprise system contracts
Human approval controls
Observability and cost controls
Evidence and audit readiness
Runbooks and support handoff
Executive command center visibility
Product Boundary
This product governs AI workflows and agent operations.
It does not claim autonomous production enforcement unless explicitly implemented and approved.
It does not replace legal, compliance, audit, or security review.
It does not mutate enterprise systems without governed approval paths.
